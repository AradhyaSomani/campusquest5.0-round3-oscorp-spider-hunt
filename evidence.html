MASKED VIGILANTE DESTROYS OSCORP LAB!
Genetically Modified Arachnid Specimen #42 Still Missing

Uptown Manhattan was thrown into chaos late Tuesday night after a masked vigilante broke into the Oscorp genetics research facility. Security footage reportedly shows the intruder moving through restricted corridors before setting off multiple containment alarms. Equipment worth an estimated twelve million dollars was destroyed in the ensuing struggle. Research staff on site say they had no warning before the attack began.

Numerous witnesses gathered outside the building as emergency crews arrived on the scene. Alarms continued blaring for nearly twenty minutes before the facility was declared secure. Machines throughout the west wing sustained heavy damage, according to an internal assessment. Evidence collected from the scene has reportedly been sealed under Oscorp's own private security division rather than handed to police.

Investigators arriving at the facility were initially denied access, sparking questions about what Oscorp may be hiding. Sources close to the case confirm that a specimen escaped containment during the chaos. Precisely what that specimen is remains unclear, though internal paperwork references “Genetically Modified Arachnid Specimen #42” as unaccounted for. Reports from within the building suggest the vigilante may have deliberately released it.

Officials at Oscorp have so far refused to comment on the nature of the missing specimen. Journalists attempting to reach spokesperson Marla Jameson were redirected to a prepared statement insisting that public safety was never at risk. Eyewitnesses near the facility described a blur crossing the rooftops moments before police arrived.

Chaos aside, questions remain about why Oscorp was running such research in the first place. Technicians who spoke on condition of anonymity say the lab has quietly expanded its cross-species genetics program over the past year. Oscorp's public relations team continues to insist that all protocols were followed. Missing from their statement, however, was any explanation for Specimen #42's disappearance.

Emergency services have since left the scene, but the investigation continues. Genetic material recovered from the lab is now being analyzed at an undisclosed location. Authorities have urged anyone with information about the vigilante or the missing specimen to contact the NYPD tip line immediately.
