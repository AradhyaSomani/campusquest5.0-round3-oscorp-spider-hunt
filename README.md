# ROUND 3 - Campus Quest 5.0
# OSCORP SPIDERMAN HUNT

Congratulations, Detectives. You've made it further than most. But the real case starts now.

Last night, Oscorp Industries suffered what their PR team is calling "a minor security incident." That's a lie. What actually happened: a masked vigilante broke into their genetics research facility, tore through a containment lab, destroyed millions of dollars in equipment — and in the chaos, something got out.

Not someone. *Something.*

Buried in Oscorp's internal paperwork is a single line that should terrify anyone who reads it: **Genetically Modified Arachnid Specimen #42 — unaccounted for.**

Oscorp tried to bury the story. They failed. A folder of leaked internal documents has found its way into your hands — a torn photograph, an intercepted memo, a garbled lab report, a newspaper clipping — sitting in a sealed envelope marked **CONFIDENTIAL: CROSS-SPECIES GENETICS.**

Somewhere in that pile of paper is everything you need to break into Oscorp's mainframe. A username. A password. But Oscorp doesn't hand out its secrets — you're going to have to pull them out letter by letter, piece by piece, out of documents that were never meant to be read by outsiders.

Not everything in that envelope matters. Oscorp's leak wasn't clean — there's noise mixed in with the signal. Real evidence sits next to distractions. Part of your job is figuring out which is which.

**Once you've cracked it** — once you have real credentials in hand — you'll sit down at the Oscorp Mainframe Login terminal and type them in. Get it wrong, and the system throws you out. Get it right, and everything changes.

The mainframe doesn't just say "welcome." It opens up. Project Omega access restored, and with it — everything Oscorp was hiding. Internal tracking data. Last known movement. Every detail their security team scrubbed from the official report. The mainframe itself will tell you where the specimen is and what you need to know to find it.

**Spider-Man is out there. And now, so are his confirmed possible location — sitting right there on your screen in the form of a riddle.**

Read it carefully. It won't be a street address — Oscorp doesn't work that way, and neither does this game. What you'll get is enough to figure out exactly where on campus to go, if you're paying attention.

The teams that crack the documents fastest, unlock the mainframe first, and reach him before anyone else — those are the teams that win Campus Quest 5.0.
